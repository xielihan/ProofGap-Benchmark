PROOF GAP @1
ASSUM:
1. z : CartesianProd(RealSet, RealSet) → RealSet
2. FuncOfClassK(z, 2)
3. forall (x) (y), x ∈ RealSet ∧ y ∈ RealSet ⇒ x + y + z(x, y) = e^{z(x, y)} ∧ e^{z(x, y)} - 1 ≠ 0

GOAL:
diff(fun x, y [x ∈ RealSet ∧ y ∈ RealSet] . x) + diff(fun x, y [x ∈ RealSet ∧ y ∈ RealSet] . y) + diff(z) = (fun x, y [x ∈ RealSet ∧ y ∈ RealSet] . e^{z(x, y)}) * diff(z)

METHOD:
[@method 两边同时求微分 @]
