forall (x), x ∈ RealSet ∧ x ≠ 0 ∧ y(x) ≠ 0 ∧ y(x)^{-frac(1, 3)} ≠ 0 ⇒ FunDeri(y, 1, 1)(x) = -frac(x^{-frac(1, 3)}, y(x)^{-frac(1, 3)})
