PROOF GAP @2
ASSUM:
1. C ⊆ CartesianProd(RealSet, CartesianProd(RealSet, RealSet))
2. x ∈ RealSet
3. y ∈ RealSet
4. z ∈ RealSet
5. s : RealSet → RealSet
6. a ∈ RealSet ∧ a > 0
7. C = { (x, y, z) | x ∈ RealSet ∧ y ∈ RealSet ∧ z ∈ RealSet ∧ x^{2} + y^{2} + z^{2} = a^{2} ∧ x + y + z = 0 }
8. ScalarCurveInt(C, (fun x, y, z [x ∈ RealSet ∧ y ∈ RealSet ∧ z ∈ RealSet] . x^{2}) * diff(s)) = ScalarCurveInt(C, (fun x, y, z [x ∈ RealSet ∧ y ∈ RealSet ∧ z ∈ RealSet] . y^{2}) * diff(s))

GOAL:
ScalarCurveInt(C, (fun x, y, z [x ∈ RealSet ∧ y ∈ RealSet ∧ z ∈ RealSet] . y^{2}) * diff(s)) = ScalarCurveInt(C, (fun x, y, z [x ∈ RealSet ∧ y ∈ RealSet ∧ z ∈ RealSet] . z^{2}) * diff(s))

[@method 根据 "对称性" @]
