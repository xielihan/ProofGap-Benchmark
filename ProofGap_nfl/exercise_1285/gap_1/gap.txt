PROOF GAP @1
ASSUM:
1. f : RealSet → RealSet
2. a ∈ RealSet
3. k ∈ RealSet ∧ k ∈ PosRealSet
4. ContinuousFuncOn(f, [a, +∞))
5. forall (x), x ∈ RealSet ∧ x ∈ IntervalLoRo(a, +∞) ⇒ FunDeri(f, 1, 1)(x) > k
6. f(a) < 0

exists (ξ), ξ ∈ RealSet ∧ ξ ∈ IntervalLoRo(a, a - frac(f(a), k)) ∧ f(a - frac(f(a), k)) - f(a) = -frac(f(a), k) * FunDeri(f, 1, 1)(ξ)
