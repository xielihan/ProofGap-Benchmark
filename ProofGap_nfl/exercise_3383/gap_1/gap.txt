PROOF GAP @1
ASSUM:
1. z : CartesianProd(RealSet, RealSet) → RealSet
2. a ∈ RealSet
3. FuncOfClassK(z, 2)
4. forall (x) (y), x ∈ RealSet ∧ y ∈ RealSet ⇒ x^{2} + y^{2} + z(x, y)^{2} = a^{2} ∧ z(x, y) ≠ 0

GOAL:
(fun x, y [x ∈ RealSet ∧ y ∈ RealSet] . x) * diff(fun x, y [x ∈ RealSet ∧ y ∈ RealSet] . x) + (fun x, y [x ∈ RealSet ∧ y ∈ RealSet] . y) * diff(fun x, y [x ∈ RealSet ∧ y ∈ RealSet] . y) + (fun x, y [x ∈ RealSet ∧ y ∈ RealSet] . z(x, y)) * diff(z) = 0

METHOD:
[@method 两边同时对 x 求微分 @]
