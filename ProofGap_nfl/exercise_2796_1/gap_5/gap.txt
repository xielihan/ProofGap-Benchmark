PROOF GAP @5
ASSUM:
1. r : NonNegIntegerSet → RealSet
2. f : RealSet → RealSet
3. S : CartesianProd(NonNegIntegerSet, RealSet) → RealSet
4. k ∈ NonNegIntegerSet

9. ConvergentSeries(sum_{ k = 1 }^{ +∞ } (frac(1, 3^{k})))
