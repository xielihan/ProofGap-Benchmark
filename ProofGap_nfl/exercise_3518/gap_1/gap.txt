PROOF GAP @1
ASSUM:
1. u : CartesianProd(RealSet, RealSet) → RealSet
2. v : CartesianProd(RealSet, RealSet) → RealSet
3. w : CartesianProd(RealSet, RealSet) → RealSet
4. z : CartesianProd(RealSet, RealSet) → RealSet
5. forall (x) (y), x ∈ RealSet ∧ y ∈ RealSet ⇒ x = sin(u(x, y)) ∧ y = sin(v(x, y)) ∧ z(x, y) = e^{w(x, y)}
6. forall (x) (y), x ∈ RealSet ∧ y ∈ RealSet ⇒ (1 - x^{2}) * FunDeri(z, x, 2)(x, y) + (1 - y^{2}) * FunDeri(z, y, 2)(x, y) - x * FunDeri(z, x, 1)(x, y) - y * FunDeri(z, y, 1)(x, y) = 0

GOAL:
forall (x) (y), x ∈ RealSet ∧ y ∈ RealSet ⇒ FunDeri(z, x, 1)(x, y) = FunDeri(z, w, 1)(x, y) * FunDeri(w, u, 1)(x, y) * FunDeri(u, x, 1)(x, y)
