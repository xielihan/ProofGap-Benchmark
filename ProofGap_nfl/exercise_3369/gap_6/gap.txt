PROOF GAP @6
ASSUM:
1. a ∈ RealSet ∧ a > 0
2. k ∈ RealSet ∧ k ≥ 0 ∧ k < 1
3. φ : RealSet → RealSet
4. φ(0) = 0
5. ContinuousFuncOn(FunDeri(φ, 1, 1), IntervalLoRo(-a, a))
6. forall (y), y ∈ RealSet ∧ y ∈ IntervalLoRo(-a, a) ⇒ |FunDeri(φ, 1, 1)(y)| ≤ k
7. F = (fun x, y [x ∈ RealSet ∧ y ∈ RealSet] . x - y - φ(y))
8. F(0, 0) = 0
9. forall (x) (y), y ∈ RealSet ∧ x ∈ RealSet ∧ y ∈ IntervalLoRo(-a, a) ⇒ ContinuousFuncAt(F, (x, y)) ∧ ContinuousFuncAt(FunDeri(F, 1, 1), (x, y)) ∧ ContinuousFuncAt(FunDeri(F, 2, 1), (x, y))
10. forall (x) (y), y ∈ RealSet ∧ x ∈ RealSet ∧ y ∈ IntervalLoRo(-a, a) ⇒ FunDeri(F, 2, 1)(x, y) = -1 - FunDeri(φ, 1, 1)(y)
11. FunDeri(F, 2, 1)(0, 0) = -1 - FunDeri(φ, 1, 1)(0)
12. |FunDeri(φ, 1, 1)(0)| ≤ k

GOAL:
k < 1
