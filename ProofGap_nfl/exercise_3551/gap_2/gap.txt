ASSUM:
1. x ∈ RealSet
2. y ∈ RealSet
3. z ∈ RealSet

5. λ ∈ RealSet
6. (2 * x, 4 * y, 6 * z) = (2 * x, 4 * y, 6 * z)

(exists (λ), λ ∈ RealSet ∧ x = λ ∧ 2 * y = 4 * λ ∧ 3 * z = 6 * λ) ⇒ (exists (r), r ∈ RealSet ∧ (2 * x, 4 * y, 6 * z) = (r, 4 * r, 6 * r))
