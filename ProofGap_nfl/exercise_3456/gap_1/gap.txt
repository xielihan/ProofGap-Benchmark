PROOF GAP @1
ASSUM:
1. x : RealSet → RealSet
2. y : RealSet → RealSet
3. r : RealSet → RealSet
4. φ : RealSet → RealSet
5. W : RealSet → RealSet
6. FuncOfClassK(x, 2)
7. FuncOfClassK(y, 2)
8. forall (t), t ∈ RealSet ⇒ x(t) ≠ 0 ∧ x(t)^{2} + y(t)^{2} > 0
9. forall (t), t ∈ RealSet ⇒ r(t) = sqrtn(2, x(t)^{2} + y(t)^{2})
10. forall (t), t ∈ RealSet ⇒ φ(t) = arctan(frac(y(t), x(t)))
11. forall (t), t ∈ RealSet ⇒ W(t) = x(t) * FunDeri(y, 1, 2)(t) - y(t) * FunDeri(x, 1, 2)(t)

GOAL:
forall (t), t ∈ RealSet ⇒ r(t) * diff(r) = x(t) * diff(x) + y(t) * diff(y)

METHOD:
[@method 由 r(t) = sqrtn(2, x(t)^{2} + y(t)^{2}) 两边同时求微分 @]
