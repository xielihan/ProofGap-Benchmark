PROOF GAP @1
ASSUM:
1. V ∈ RealSet ∧ V ∈ PosRealSet
2. x ∈ RealSet ∧ x ∈ PosRealSet
3. y ∈ RealSet ∧ y ∈ PosRealSet
4. h ∈ RealSet ∧ h ∈ PosRealSet
5. S : CartesianProd(RealSet, CartesianProd(RealSet, RealSet)) → RealSet
6. F : CartesianProd(RealSet, CartesianProd(RealSet, CartesianProd(RealSet, RealSet))) → RealSet
7. λ ∈ RealSet
8. x * y * h = V
9. forall (u) (v) (w), u ∈ RealSet ∧ v ∈ RealSet ∧ w ∈ RealSet ⇒ S(u, v, w) = 2 * (u + v) * w + u * v
10. F = (fun x, y, h, λ [x ∈ RealSet ∧ y ∈ RealSet ∧ h ∈ RealSet ∧ λ ∈ RealSet] . S(x, y, h) - λ * (x * y * h - V))

GOAL:
FunDeri(F, 1, 1) = y + 2 * h - λ * y * h

METHOD:
