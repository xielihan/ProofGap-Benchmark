PROOF GAP @4
ASSUM:
1. y : RealSet → RealSet
2. forall (x), x ∈ RealSet ⇒ y(x) = e^{-x^{2}}
3. forall (x), x ∈ RealSet ⇒ FunDeri(y, 1, 1)(x) = -2 * x * e^{-x^{2}}
4. forall (x), x ∈ RealSet ⇒ FunDeri(y, 1, 2)(x) = e^{-x^{2}} * (4 * x^{2} - 2)
5. forall (x), x ∈ RealSet ∧ |x| < frac(1, sqrtn(2, 2)) ⇒ FunDeri(y, 1, 2)(x) < 0

GOAL:
ConcaveFuncOn(y, IntervalLoRo(-frac(1, sqrtn(2, 2)), frac(1, sqrtn(2, 2))))
