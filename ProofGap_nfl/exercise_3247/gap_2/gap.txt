PROOF GAP @2
ASSUM:
1. A : CartesianProd(RealSet, RealSet) → RealSet
2. R ∈ RealSet
3. α ∈ RealSet
4. R = 20
5. α = frac(π, 3)
6. diff(α) = frac(π, 180)
7. A(R, α) = frac(1, 2) * R^{2} * α
8. forall (ε), ε ∈ RealSet ∧ ε > 0 ⇒ diff(A) ≈_{ ε } (fun R, α [R ∈ RealSet ∧ α ∈ RealSet] . R * α) * diff(fun R, α [R ∈ RealSet ∧ α ∈ RealSet] . R) + (fun R, α [R ∈ RealSet ∧ α ∈ RealSet] . frac(1, 2) * R^{2}) * diff(fun R, α [R ∈ RealSet ∧ α ∈ RealSet] . α)

GOAL:
forall (ε), ε ∈ RealSet ∧ ε > 0 ⇒ 20 * frac(π, 3) * diff(fun R, α [R ∈ RealSet ∧ α ∈ RealSet] . R) + frac(1, 2) * 20^{2} * frac(π, 180) ≈_{ ε } 0

METHOD:
[@method 代入 R = 20, α = frac(π, 3), diff(α) = frac(π, 180) 到 diff(A) ≈_{ ε } R * α * diff(R) + frac(1, 2) * R^{2} * diff(α) @]
