8. f(a) = 0

forall (Δx), Δx ∈ RealSet ∧ Δx ≠ 0 ∧ (Δx > -δ ∧ Δx < δ) ⇒ frac(f(a + Δx) - f(a), Δx) = frac(|Δx|, Δx) * φ(a + Δx)
