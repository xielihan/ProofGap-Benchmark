PROOF GAP @1
ASSUM:
1. a ∈ RealSet
2. b ∈ RealSet
3. x : RealSet → RealSet
4. y : RealSet → RealSet
5. s ∈ RealSet
6. ε ∈ RealSet
7. a = 1
8. b = frac(1, 2)
9. forall (t), t ∈ RealSet ⇒ x(t) = a * sin(t)
10. forall (t), t ∈ RealSet ⇒ y(t) = b * cos(t)
11. ε = frac(sqrtn(2, a^{2} - b^{2}), a)
12. s = DefInt(0, 2 * π, (fun t [t ∈ RealSet ∧ 0 ≤ t ∧ t ≤ 2 * π] . sqrtn(2, FunDeri(x, 1, 1)(t)^{2} + FunDeri(y, 1, 1)(t)^{2})) * diff(fun t [t ∈ RealSet] . t))

GOAL:
forall (t), t ∈ RealSet ⇒ FunDeri(x, 1, 1)(t) = a * cos(t) ∧ FunDeri(y, 1, 1)(t) = -b * sin(t)

METHOD:
