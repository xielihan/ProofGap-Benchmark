11. forall (x) (y) (z) (λ), x ∈ RealSet ∧ y ∈ RealSet ∧ z ∈ RealSet ∧ λ ∈ RealSet ⇒ F(x, y, z, λ) = x * y * (c - z) - λ * (frac(x^{2}, a^{2}) + frac(y^{2}, b^{2}) - frac(z, c))
