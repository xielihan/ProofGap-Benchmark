ASSUM:
1. a ∈ RealSet
2. A ∈ RealSet
3. b ∈ RealSet
4. B ∈ RealSet
5. p ∈ RealSet
6. φ : CartesianProd(RealSet, RealSet) → RealSet
7. f : RealSet → RealSet
8. F : RealSet → RealSet
9. R ⊆ CartesianProd(RealSet, RealSet)
10. a < A
11. b < B

14. ContinuousFuncOn(φ, R)
