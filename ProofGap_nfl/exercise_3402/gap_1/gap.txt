PROOF GAP @1
ASSUM:
1. x : RealSet → RealSet
2. y : RealSet → RealSet
3. exists (δ), δ ∈ RealSet ∧ δ > 0 ∧ DiffableFuncOn(x, IntervalLoRo(2 - δ, 2 + δ))
4. exists (δ), δ ∈ RealSet ∧ δ > 0 ∧ DiffableFuncOn(y, IntervalLoRo(2 - δ, 2 + δ))
5. forall (z), z ∈ RealSet ⇒ x(z)^{2} + y(z)^{2} = frac(1, 2) * z^{2}
6. forall (z), z ∈ RealSet ⇒ x(z) + y(z) + z = 2
7. x(2) = 1
8. y(2) = -1

GOAL:
forall (z), z ∈ RealSet ⇒ 2 * x(z) * FunDeri(x, 1, 1)(z) + 2 * y(z) * FunDeri(y, 1, 1)(z) = z
