ASSUM:
1. a ∈ RealSet ∧ a > 0

forall (x), x ∈ RealSet ∧ 0 < x ∧ x < a ⇒ FunDeri(f, 1, 1)(x) = x^{m - 1} * (a - x)^{n - 1} * (m * a - (m + n) * x)
