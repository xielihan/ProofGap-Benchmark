PROOF GAP @1
ASSUM:
1. f : RealSet → RealSet
2. a ∈ RealSet
3. b ∈ RealSet
4. ξ ∈ RealSet ∧ ξ ∈ IntervalLoRo(a, b)
5. a < b
6. FuncOfClassKOn(f, IntervalLoRo(a, b), 2)
7. FunDeri(f, 1, 2)(ξ) ≠ 0

GOAL:
FunDeri(f, 1, 2)(ξ) > 0 ⇒ FunDeri(f, 1, 1)(ξ) = 0 ⇒ ξ ∈ MinimumPointOn(f, IntervalLoRo(a, b))
