PROOF GAP @2
ASSUM:
1. a ∈ RealSet
2. k ∈ RealSet
3. s ∈ RealSet
4. v ∈ RealSet
5. Q : RealSet → RealSet
6. a > 0
7. k > 0
8. s > 0
9. v > 0
10. forall (v), v ∈ RealSet ∧ v > 0 ⇒ Q(v) = (a + k * v^{3}) * frac(s, v)
11. forall (v), v ∈ RealSet ∧ v > 0 ⇒ Q(v) = frac(a * s, v) + s * k * v^{2}

GOAL:
FunDeri(Q, 1, 1)(v) = -frac(a * s, v^{2}) + 2 * s * k * v

METHOD:
